Questions.

Why is there no skip button?

a skip button is a patch for badly timed breaks, so iris fixes the timing instead. it holds a break while you are typing or in a fullscreen app, and only lets it land once you stop. by the time the screen goes dark, you were between things anyway. that is also why the 20 seconds actually happen.

Does it work with more than one monitor?

yes. the break covers every connected display, not just the one your cursor is on. a dark laptop screen next to a bright external one would defeat the point.

Does the camera thing send anything anywhere?

no. posture detection runs on your Mac through Apple's Vision framework. the frame is never stored and never uploaded, and there is no server on the other end of it. it is also off until you turn it on.

What makes this different from other break reminders?

it also blocks the apps and sites that pull you away while you work. every other break app handles your eyes, every blocker handles your attention, and nothing else does both. that is the whole reason iris exists.

Will it interrupt me during a meeting?

no. it pauses when your camera is in use, which covers calls in a browser tab as well as the usual apps. for anything else, meetings without video, a lunch break, quiet hours, you can set a schedule block and iris stays out of the way.

When can I get it?

it is not finished, so there is no date to give you. the waitlist above is the whole plan: you leave an email, and you get one message on the day it is ready to download. no newsletter in between.
