Questions.

Q

Why is there no skip button?

because a break you can skip is a break you'll skip every single time. the 20 seconds only do anything if they actually happen.

Q

macOS says Iris is damaged. What do I do?

nothing's wrong with the file, it just means the app isn't signed yet. run this in Terminal and it'll open normally: xattr -d com.apple.quarantine ~/Downloads/Iris.dmg
