How it works

The 20-20-20 rule,
on autopilot.

01

Install & forget

Download EyeRest. It lives quietly in your menu bar (Mac) or system tray (Windows) — no Dock icon, no clutter.

02

Warning banner drops

A slim notification slides down 2 minutes before your break. You can dismiss it or let it time out naturally.

03

Screen goes black

Every display goes completely dark for 20 seconds. Look out a window, stand up, blink — let your eyes reset.

04

Back to work

The screen fades back in automatically. The timer resets and the cycle begins again.
